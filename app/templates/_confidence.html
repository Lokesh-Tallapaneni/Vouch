{# The route-confidence badge, in its own file so callers can import the macro without executing _chain.html's top-level markup (which needs a `route` in context and would raise on import). Route confidence is the product of tie strengths along a whole chain, so it lives on a different scale from any single hop. Bands are calibrated against the measured spread across the eight seeded companies (0.20-0.88): strong >= 0.60, medium >= 0.40, weak below. Do not reuse the per-hop thresholds (0.70 / 0.45) here -- they measure a different quantity, and reusing them would put nearly every route in one band. #} {% macro confidence_badge(value) -%} {{ '%.2f'|format(value) }} {%- endmacro %}
